How It Works
01

Automated Collection

Every two weeks, a GitHub Actions workflow fetches articles from 29 RSS feeds across games, art, AI, TTRPG, and film sources. Each article is scored 1–10 for relevance by Claude Haiku. Articles scoring 5 or higher are saved to data/stories.json and a notification email is sent to the team.

02

Gate 1 — Story Review

Review the scored articles one by one. Approve the ones worth including in the issue and reject the rest. Approved stories are saved to data/gate1-decisions.json and grouped by section. Aim for 3–8 approved stories across all sections before moving to Gate 2.

03

Gate 2 — Draft & Publish

Trigger the draft workflow to generate full editorial copy for each section using Claude Sonnet, with a strategic gap scan appended. Review section by section, approve each, then hit Publish. The issue is written into index.html and saved as a standalone issue-NNN.html, and data/archive.json is updated automatically.

04

Published & Live

The live site updates automatically via GitHub Pages within ~60 seconds of publishing. Past issues are permanently accessible from the Archive. The next collection cycle begins automatically in two weeks, or you can trigger it manually from the pipeline above.
